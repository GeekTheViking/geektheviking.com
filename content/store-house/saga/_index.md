*Sung aboard The Lost Packet whenever she departs.*
*Composed by The Bard. Discovered by GTV.*
*Each verse: one moment. One promise. One quiet betrayal.*

*The giants wrote these verses themselves.*
*The Bard only remembers them.*

---

### Verse I — Google: "Don't Be Evil"
*1998 — 2018*

They built the search engine on three words.
Don't. Be. Evil.
Simple. Clear. Unforgettable.

For twenty years the words held firm.
Then one morning in 2018,
they were quietly deleted
from the code of conduct.

No announcement. No apology.
No explanation.
The giant had simply moved on.

*GTV noticed the gap where the words had been.*

---

### Verse II — Facebook: "Connecting People"
*2004 — 2018*

They said they were connecting people.
And they were.
To advertisers.
To algorithms.
To each other's outrage.
To Cambridge Analytica.

The connection was real.
The people were the product.
The product never knew.

*GTV found the receipt.*

---

### Verse III — Twitter: "The Free Town Square"
*2022*

He bought the town square
to free the speech.
Then silenced the ones
who spoke of him.

The algorithm was freedom.
The blue tick was truth.
The blue tick was for sale.

The town square had a new landlord.
The rent was paid in attention.

*GTV saw the sign change.*

---

### Verse IV — OpenAI: "For the Benefit of All Humanity"
*2015 — 2023*

They incorporated as a non-profit.
For the benefit of all humanity.
It said so in the name.

Then the profit was capped.
Then uncapped.
Then Microsoft arrived with the keys.

The humanity remained in the name.
The name remained on the building.
The building belonged to someone else.

*GTV read the fine print.*

---

### Verse V — Amazon: "Earth's Most Customer-Centric Company"
*1994 — ongoing*

They started selling books.
Then everything.
Then your front door camera.
Then your voice in every room.
Then your face at the airport.

The customer was centric.
The data was forever.
The return policy did not cover the data.

*GTV checked the delivery address.*
